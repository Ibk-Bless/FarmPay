# Code of Conduct

FarmPay is built for farmers, buyers and cooperatives around the world, and we want contributing to it to feel the same way: welcoming to everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to Stellar, Rust or open source
- Give feedback on code, not on people
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming
- Credit others' work and ideas

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal attacks or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information
- Claiming issues you don't intend to work on, or submitting low-effort work only to collect rewards

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, unassign issues, or block contributors who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
